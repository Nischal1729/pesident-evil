# GJBC (Golden Jubilee Block) — levels, front ramp, 2-wheeler parking, interiors

Source: the user-supplied GJB walkthrough "GJB tour" (YouTube `UcDUlC0mBY4`, 12:15, 720p AV1, sunny monsoon day),
saved as `reference/video/gjb_tour.mp4`. Cross-checked against `reference/CAMPUS_NOTES.md`, the 2026 campus tour
(`reference/video/tour.mp4`, frames `reference/frames/key_*`) and the satellite/OSM images in `reference/`.

**Frame:** metres, Y up, +X east, +Z south, origin = OSM GJBC centroid (same as CAMPUS_NOTES.md).
**Confidence:** H = seen clearly, several angles. M = seen, position estimated. L = inferred.

Frames:
* `reference/frames/gjb/sheets/sheet_<mmss>.jpg`: 23 contact sheets. Each is 4×4 tiles, one tile every 2 s, read
  row-major, covering 32 s from `<mmss>`.
* `reference/frames/gjb/key_<mmss>_<slug>.jpg`: 36 full-res key frames. The index is in §8.

## 1. Level structure (G vs L1)

| Level | y (m) | What is on it | Conf. |
|---|---|---|---|
| **G** (ground) | 0 | PES University Rd and the **drive-through** under the NE porch; the entry road, the south walkway, the **east forecourt / bus bay**, the **east promenade** and the east lawn; the **east entrance lobby** (7 granite steps up, ≈1 m plinth); the **cafeteria** under the Law terrace; the north G colonnade along the road (west part); the lower 2-wheeler parking floor | H |
| **L1** (1st floor) | **≈6.0** (`GJB_L1`) | the **front-ramp landing** and the **NE porch** over the drive-through (inside the terracotta porte-cochère frame), the **covered plaza**, the **Quad** with its double-height colonnades, the yellow-column hall, the L1 halls behind the porte-cochère, the admission level | H (that it is L1), M (height) |
| Law terrace | ≈8 | unchanged (east-facing terrace, own granite stair) | M |

* **Height of L1.** The drive-through clearance is ≈5–5.5 m (old tour 0540: people ≈100 px tall, soffit ≈290 px
  above them). The grey planter band on the porte-cochère is the L1 parapet: its bottom edge is at the drive-through
  soffit and its top is ≈1.1 m above the L1 floor. At 2:04 the camera stands on the ramp landing with the planter
  band at eye level and the dark drive-through mouth below it. L1 ≈ 6.0 m (M).
* **Road vs Quad.** PES University Rd runs at G along the north side of GJBC. The covered plaza and its steel
  pergola sit on L1 and **look down** 6 m onto the road, the buses and B-Block (old tour 1034 and 13:04 agree). The
  drive-through (x 55→84) passes under the NE L1 porch. H.
* The Quad is **not** at the level of the road or the east forecourt. You reach it from the ramp landing through the
  porch and the covered plaza (2:06 → 2:30), or by the internal stairs. From 2:22 there are 2 granite steps up into
  the covered-plaza floor. H.
* Under the Quad and the covered plaza is the ground floor (G). In the game this is a solid podium; only its
  outward faces are modelled (§6).
* Floor-to-floor above L1 ≈4.2–4.5 m. The Quad colonnade is double height (≈8.5 m on L1), with 2–3 cream floors
  plus a glass top band above it. M.

## 2. The front ramp

* **Start:** the east plaza, north of the entry road's west end, in line with the PES Lawn promenade (old tour 0208:
  the camera on the promenade looks straight up the ramp; new 0:36 and 0:52: from the south walkway the ramp is to
  the right of (north of) the drive-through mouth, rising west). Foot ≈(110, −134). M.
* **Route:** straight, rising **west** along z ≈ −136.3…−131.9. The paving is striped buff/grey pavers. It has
  **solid dark-grey parapet walls** (1.1 m, granite coping) that are solid to the ground on the outside, and recessed
  step lights. Frangipani and the gold globe are to its north (right). H (look), M (alignment).
* **Length/slope:** ≈24 m run for 6 m rise (≈1:4, 14°). The real ramp reads steep in 0208 and 1:52–1:56. M.
* **Landing:** on **L1** at x 78.9…86, beside the north leg of the terracotta porte-cochère frame (1:56–2:06). From
  here: a granite stair goes up to L2 next to a **navy wall with the founders' bronze statue** (two seated figures on
  a granite bench, with Heliconia planters). West/south-west is the L1 porch → covered plaza ("PEOPLES EDUCATION
  SOCIETY" is visible through the frame at 1:58) → Quad. H.
* **PES signboard:** yes, the landing is under it. The big white "PES UNIVERSITY" box sits on a navy-fascia bridge
  between the grey block at the landing and MRD's refurbished grey SE block (0:58, 1:54). Below the sign is a
  glazed volume/canopy, and the view under it (0:52) shows trees across the MRD loop road. In the game the landing
  continues west as a glazed L1 bridge over the MRD loop road into MRD. The connection is M; the exact glazing
  split is L.
* The ramp does **not** rise from the south walkway. The walkway runs west at G, alongside the parking, to the bus
  bay / east forecourt. H.

## 4. The 2-wheeler parking

**Revised after the user's review.** The user's Google Maps screenshot (`reference/sat_user_parking_gmaps.webp`) and our
aligned satellite crop with a 10 m grid (`reference/sat_east_grid.jpg`) settle the placement. The first version (an
E–W strip along the north edge of the lawn) was wrong.

* **Placement (H):** a long **N–S** building **right opposite GJBC's east wing, with the east lawn between them**. It
  runs from just south of the entry walkway (z≈−111) to the food court (z≈−30). The footprint is x≈120…137.5
  (≈81 × 17.5 m). Its back (east) wall is the **campus boundary**; the neighbourhood houses start at x≈141. From
  z≈−30 to +12 the campus widens again around the South Thindies food point and the HPC lab (x≈121…150).
* **The east lawn is only x≈100…118**, between the east promenade and the parking. It is not the 53 m-wide lawn in the
  old layout, and the "corten + green-mesh boundary" in CAMPUS_NOTES (old tour 1654, 1808, 1812) is the parking's
  **lawn-facing west side**. The GJB tour at 1:10 shows the same thing: the corten/green panel wall runs N–S on the
  left, F-Block is at the far end, and the lawn is on the right. The white low walls stand in front of it.
* **Two floors (H):** the lower floor and an open upper deck with painted bays. The **white flat-roofed covered bay**
  is at the **north end**, open towards the walkway (0:40, 0:47; old tour 1922). Tall corten screens with leaf
  cut-outs stand at the entrances.
* **Access (M):** the vehicle lane runs off the entry walkway at the **north-east** end, between the parking and a
  green service block at the boundary (0:43–0:45, "17" post). The north face is painted green (2:02 and 3:16 show
  "the green wall with bikes on top" from the L1 landing and window). In reality the lower floor is partly sunk
  below the walkway. The game world is flat, so the game uses G = 0 and a deck at +3.2 m, with the vehicle ramp at
  the NE rising south, a pedestrian door in the north face, two doors in the corten face and an open south end with
  a stair up to the deck.
* **Reflecting pool (H):** at the **north end of the lawn**, E–W, under the tall dark-grey retaining wall of the
  entry walkway. The pool points at GJBC's east face (1:04–1:08; satellite: the rectangle at x≈98…115,
  z≈−108…−105).
* **Fill:** densely packed scooters and motorbikes on both levels in facing rows. The game uses ≈155 instanced bikes,
  in clusters that are densest near the walkway end.

## 6. Materials and signage (additions to CAMPUS_NOTES §3.1)

* Podium ground faces along PES Univ Rd: white plaster wall, low dark **slot windows**, granite skirting, **red
  fire-extinguisher cabinets**, and a charcoal L1 slab fascia. Above them sit the L1 grey planter parapet and a dense
  hedge. The drive-through wall matches (old 0540).
* The porte-cochère frame is dark walnut/terracotta cladding around the L1 porch opening. The grey planter band
  (L1 parapet) has lime shrubs, the "A"/"B" block signs and an orange PES banner.
* The ramp has dark-grey (#55595e) parapets with a charcoal granite coping, buff striped pavers and step lights.
* Founders' statue: bronze figures, speckled granite bench, **navy wall** (#2a3a66).
* MRD's SE block is now finished in **grey composite panels with navy canopies**. It carries the PES sign bridge.
  This contradicts the scaffolded MRD in CAMPUS_NOTES and is for the MRD agent.
* 2-wheeler parking: green paint (#3f8566), grey concrete (#9c9a94 / dark #6e6f70), white bay (#f1f1ee), corten.
* Quad: violet/purple event banners replace the blue ones on some columns during events (6:00); black cube planters
  with fiddle-leaf figs and palms.

## 9. What becomes walkable once multi-level movement lands

Everything below is already registered "the way it physically is" (see the multi-level contract):

* `podium` prism (0 → L1): its top is the **Quad / covered plaza / inner court** floor on L1. Colonnade columns,
  pergola posts and desks are prisms/circles on it; parapets on the L1 edges are prisms with base = L1.
* `ramp` (addRamp 0 → L1, x 110 → 86): the **front ramp**. Then the `landing` slab (base 5.5, top L1), the NE porch
  `deck` (base 5.5, top L1), the porch roof (base 11.2), the glazed MRD `bridge` (base 5.4) and the sign `canopy`.
* Lobby `stair` (addRamp 0 → L1) plus its top `landing`: G lobby → L1 (the arcade above needs an opening when
  levels land).
* Parking: vehicle `ramp` at the NE (0 → 3.2) and `stair` at the SW (0 → 3.2) up to the **upper deck** (`deck` slab,
  base 2.85, top 3.2), with parapets, bay posts and deck bike rows (base 3.2).
* Law terrace `stair` (now an addRamp 0 → 8).
* Props lifted to L1 (Quad benches, bins, coolers) are decor for now and need collision at L1 then (TODO in
  Props.ts `courtyard()`).
